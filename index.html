DECISION ARENA · LIVE ON THE Z13

Three open judges walk into a tablet.

The top free models from the Image JevBench post (leaderboard), downloaded to Jacob's ROG Flow Z13 and run head-to-head on eight probes: six image questions, two text-only. Each judge sees lettered options and answers with a single letter; bars are softmax probabilities over the letters from llama.cpp logprobs - the typed-decision readout these models are trained for. One run, temperature 0, llama.cpp b11160 Vulkan.
